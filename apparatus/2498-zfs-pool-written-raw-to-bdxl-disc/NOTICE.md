# BIP39 English wordlist for the pool passphrase

`bip39_english.txt` is the English word list of BIP-0039: 2,048 words, one per line, in the
order that fixes each word's 11-bit index. It is a verbatim copy.

- Source: https://github.com/bitcoin/bips/blob/master/bip-0039/english.txt
- Specification: https://github.com/bitcoin/bips/blob/master/bip-0039.mediawiki
- License: the specification states "This BIP falls under the MIT License."
- SHA-256: `2f5eed53a4727b4bf8880d8f3f199efc90e58503646d9ff8eff3a2ed3b24dbda`

Twenty-four words carry 256 bits, 264 with the checksum bits that a generator adds.
